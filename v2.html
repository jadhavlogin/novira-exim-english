Is this book for complete beginners?

Yes. It starts from the basics and walks you step by step until your first export shipment. No prior experience is needed.

Which language is the book in?

Simple, easy-to-understand English.

Is it an eBook or a printed book?

It is a printed hard copy, delivered to your address.

Can I start with a low budget?

Yes. There is a dedicated section on low-budget options, cost estimation and planning so you can start small.

Does it cover agricultural exports?

Yes — the agri-export process, packaging, required documents and finding foreign buyers for farm produce.

Can I do this alongside my job?

Yes. Many exporters start part-time. The book helps you plan your first shipment at your own pace.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on your location. You'll receive order and shipping details after purchase.
